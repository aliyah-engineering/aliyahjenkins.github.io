ABOUT ME

A multidisciplinary scientist moving from molecular systems into advanced materials and aerospace engineering.

My Background

I earned my bachelor's degree in biochemistry from Arizona State University, where I developed a strong foundation in biochemical research, structural biology, chromatography, protein purification, spectroscopy, and scientific analysis.

My experience later expanded into battery research and development, materials characterization, microscopy, metrology, manufacturing support, failure analysis, and technical documentation.

I am pursuing graduate education in materials science and engineering to connect my molecular-science background with advanced engineering applications.

My Direction

My long-term goal is to contribute to aerospace and defense technologies through advanced materials, failure analysis, reliability engineering, computational modeling, and machine learning.

I am especially interested in thermal degradation, functional materials, thin films, stealth materials, metamaterials, energy systems, and physics-informed machine learning.
